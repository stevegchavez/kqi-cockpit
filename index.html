      Privacy and safety

      Searching and routing use OpenStreetMap services (Nominatim and routing.openstreetmap.de). They are sent what you type, your start and end points, and, to favour nearby results, a rough 100 km box around where you are. Nothing is sent until you tap. Routes are for bicycles, which keeps you off motorways but can include paths where scooters are not allowed, so follow local rules and signs. Directions are spoken when Voice is on (using the phone's built-in speech, nothing is sent anywhere) and need the screen on: keep the phone in a mount and only glance when it is safe. Saved places and your parking spot are stored only in this browser.

              Setup

              Scooter keys

              This app talks to your scooter with two secrets NIU assigns to it: its Bluetooth password and AES key. They are stored only in this browser, on this device.

              Get them once with the open-source niu-kqi tool, which logs in to NIU on your computer and saves them to secrets/scooter.json. Paste that file's contents below, or paste the password and the AES key on two lines.
